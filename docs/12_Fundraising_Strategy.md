# 12. Fundraising Strategy & Stewardship

## Campaign Pillars
1. **Infrastructure & Capital Projects**: Healthcare wing expansion, solar energy installations.
2. **Emergency Welfare Funds**: Seasonal food baskets, winter blanket drives.
3. **Youth & Education Scholarships**: Laptop drives, academic grants.

## Transparency Controls
- Public progress bars on active campaign cards.
- Automated reference transaction receipt numbers (`TXN-2026-90XXX`).
- Monthly public stewardship financial dashboard updates.
