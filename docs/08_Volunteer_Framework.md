# 08. Volunteer Framework & Recognition System

## Volunteer Lifecycle
1. **Intake & Profiling**: Skill tagging, availability registration.
2. **Onboarding & Safety Orientation**: Mandatory background verification and ethics review.
3. **Committee Assignment**: Task matching based on skills and preferences.
4. **Hours Logging**: Digital hour submission and administrator approval.
5. **Recognition Levels**:
   - **Bronze Star**: 1 - 49 Hours
   - **Silver Star Achiever**: 50 - 99 Hours
   - **Gold Star Leadership**: 100+ Hours
