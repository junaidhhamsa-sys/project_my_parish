# 07. Community Governance Framework

## 45-Day Framework Alignment
The platform operationalizes the 45-Day Framework:
- **Days 1–5**: Architecture & Requirements alignment.
- **Days 6–15**: Committee leadership & governance structure.
- **Days 16-[25]**: Community services digital transformation.
- **Days 26–35**: SOP standardization & competitive benchmarking.
- **Days 36–45**: KPI tracking, financial sustainability, and framework presentation.

## Governance Org Chart
- **Pastoral Council**: Strategic oversight.
- **Secretariat**: Operational execution & audit compliance.
- **Standing Committees**: Youth, Education, Healthcare, Welfare, Women Development, Finance.
