# 09. Community Services Framework

## Service Sectors
1. **Healthcare & Wellness**: Free clinics, medical screening, blood pressure monitoring.
2. **Education Support**: After-school tutoring, adult literacy, digital apprenticeships.
3. **Welfare Relief**: Emergency food baskets, utility micro-grants, senior care.
4. **Counselling & Mental Health**: Family guidance, grief support, recovery groups.
5. **Women Development**: Vocational skills, tailoring, micro-business workshops.
