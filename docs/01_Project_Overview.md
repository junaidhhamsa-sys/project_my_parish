# 01. Project Overview — MY PARISH MY PROUD

## Executive Summary
**MY PARISH MY PROUD** is an enterprise-grade SaaS community development and parish management platform built to digitally transform parish operations, member services, volunteer management, emergency welfare relief, and financial stewardship.

Based on the **45-Day Community Development Framework**, the platform unifies administrators, committee leaders, volunteers, and parishioners into a single transparent digital ecosystem.

## Core Strategic Objectives
1. **Centralized Member Management**: Group members into household units, track participation history, and enable digital self-service.
2. **Volunteer Optimization**: Mobilize volunteers across 7 specialized committees, track hours logged, and recognize top contributors.
3. **Compassionate Welfare Relief**: Confidential intake of emergency assistance requests with strict role-based authorization and audit logs.
4. **Transparent Stewardship**: Track fundraising campaigns, generate double-entry transaction receipts, and display real-time progress metrics.
5. **AI Community Intelligence**: Controlled natural language assistant allowing leaders to query real-time parish statistics.

## Key Stakeholders
- **Parish Leadership**: Fathers, Sisters, Pastoral Council.
- **Committee Leaders**: Youth, Education, Healthcare, Welfare, Women Development, Finance.
- **Volunteers & Members**: Parishioners accessing community services and participating in events.
